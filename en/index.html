05 / FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Before we meet.

Can I come without knowing Python?

Yes. If you already have a manual testing foundation, we’ll start with Python basics and move toward automated tests. If you’re just getting into QA, we’ll first discuss your current level and a suitable path.

How much does it cost?

The price depends on the format and scope: a consultation, a review, or ongoing support. After we meet, we’ll agree on the price and the work before sessions start.

How often will we meet?

We’ll agree on frequency and length based on your goal, schedule, and time for practice. For a one-off problem, we can start with a single consultation.

Can we work through tasks from my job?

Yes, if you have the right to share the materials. Anonymized examples without keys, personal data, or confidential company information work well.

Will you help me prepare for interviews?

We can review your experience and CV, practice technical questions, and identify topics to study. Whether you get the job depends on your skills, the role requirements, and the employer’s decision.

Will there be homework between sessions?

In ongoing mentorship — if we agree on it. We’ll pick practice you can actually finish and review the result. We’ll agree on timing and the feedback channel in advance.
